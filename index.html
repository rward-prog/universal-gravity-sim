How to use Gravity Lab

Add a body: pick one above, then click empty space.

Launch it: click, hold, and drag. The arrow shows where it will be after one second on your screen. Let go to launch.

Change a body: click it, then use the Body panel. Drag from a body to give it a new velocity.

Move and zoom: mouse wheel to zoom. Hold Shift and drag, or press Move view, to slide the view.

Keys: Space pauses. ← and → move time backward and forward (tap for a nudge, hold to scrub, Shift to go faster). ↑ and ↓ change the mass of the selected body. F toggles full screen and Esc leaves it.

Numbers: turn on Real numbers for mass, distance, force, acceleration, and g.

Forces: turn on Force arrows for one arrow per pull plus the net force. Arrow lengths are logarithmic, so they are not proportional to the force.

Newton's third law: turn on Pair view.
